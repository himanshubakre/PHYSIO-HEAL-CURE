About the clinic

An evidence-based physiotherapist, close to home.

Physio Heal & Cure by S.S is run out of Kausa, Mumbra, with a female physiotherapist known locally for her personal, patient approach — also practising as True Heal Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation Center, Almas Colony, Kausa.

Every plan starts with a proper assessment, not a standard routine. Whether it's a stiff neck from a desk job or recovery after surgery, treatment is adjusted session to session based on how your body actually responds.

  • One-on-one sessions, not batch treatment
  • Home visits across Kausa & Mumbra
  • Exercise plans you can continue between visits
